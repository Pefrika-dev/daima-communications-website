Production requirements

What must change before real use.

  • Secure server-side authentication and role-based authorization.
  • A proper database with validated, permissioned APIs.
  • Server-side input validation and rate limiting.
  • HTTPS, security headers and CSRF protection.
  • Audit logs, backups and a data-retention policy.
  • Consent, privacy and data-subject request handling.
  • Verified business details, pricing and stock feeds.

This dashboard intentionally has no fake login screen. A client-side password would provide no real security and could mislead users.
