Useful answers

A few practical answers about the demonstration catalogue, branch details and inquiries.

What products can I find on the website?+

The demonstration catalogue groups products into smartphones, feature phones, chargers and cables, earphones and headphones, cases and protection, power banks, smartwatches, speakers, computer accessories and other electronics. These are proposed categories; not every category is confirmed as currently stocked.

How can I confirm the latest price?+

Prices shown here are illustrative demonstration prices only. Contact the relevant branch — once a verified phone or WhatsApp number is configured — and quote the product name and model. Confirm the exact price before making a purchase.

How do I check whether a product is available?+

Use the “Ask on WhatsApp” action where configured, or submit the demonstration inquiry form. No branch stock is verified in this concept, so every listing asks you to contact the branch to confirm availability.

How do I find the Sirare branch?+

Open the Sirare branch details and use the directions action, which opens a Google Maps search for the Sirare / Isebania area. The exact street address has not been supplied and is marked “to be confirmed” — call or message before travelling.

How do I find the Kehancha branch?+

Open the Kehancha branch details and use the directions action to search Kehancha town. As with Sirare, the precise address, hours and phone number are placeholders pending confirmation from the business.

Can I inquire about a product through WhatsApp?+

Yes — when a verified business WhatsApp number is configured. The website builds a prefilled message containing the product, model, demonstration price and preferred branch so the conversation starts clearly. Until a number is added, WhatsApp buttons remain disabled and the configuration note is visible.
