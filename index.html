What products can I find on the website?+

The catalogue groups products into smartphones, feature phones, chargers and cables, earphones and headphones, cases and protection, power banks, smartwatches, speakers, computer accessories and other electronics. The range can vary by branch, so call or WhatsApp to confirm the model you want.

How can I confirm the latest price?+

Prices shown online are indicative. Call or WhatsApp 0701 127 577, quote the product name and model, and the team will confirm today's price before you pay.

How do I check whether a product is available?+

Call or WhatsApp 0701 127 577 with the product name and preferred branch. The team will confirm whether it is available in Sirare or Kehancha. The website does not show live branch stock.

How do I find the Sirare branch?+

Open the Sirare branch details to see the area and start a directions search. Call 0701 127 577 for exact directions and today's opening hours before travelling.

How do I find the Kehancha branch?+

Open the Kehancha branch details and use the directions action to search Kehancha town. Call 0701 127 577 for exact directions and current opening hours.

Can I inquire about a product through WhatsApp?+

Yes. Call or WhatsApp 0701 127 577. The same number serves both branches, and the website can prepare a message with the product, preferred branch and your question so the conversation starts clearly.
